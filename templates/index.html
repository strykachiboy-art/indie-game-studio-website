About the studio

StrykaMax Studio is a solo game development studio where every system, artwork, mechanic, and line of code is created by one developer with a passion for immersive experiences. It started as a way to learn by shipping — small prototypes, weekend experiments, and unfinished ideas that slowly turned into finished games.

There's no studio behind the name yet, just a developer who treats every bug fixed and every mechanic tuned as progress worth sharing. The goal isn't to look big — it's to build things that are honest, playable, and genuinely fun.
