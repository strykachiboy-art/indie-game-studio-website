StrykaMax Studio isn't a commercial company — it's a transparent portfolio and devlog tracking my journey into game development. Every mechanic and line of code is written by me as I figure out how game loops, graphics rendering, and logic work.

Instead of hiding behind a fake corporate "we", this site embraces the solo reality: celebrating small milestones, posting messy prototypes, and sharing real progress.
