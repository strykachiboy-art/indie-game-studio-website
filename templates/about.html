My story

About the studio

StrykaMax Studio started in 2026 as a solo project — no team, no office, just one developer learning Python and Flask by building things instead of just reading about them. It's small on purpose, because small is what lets every part of it get real attention.

Read full story... Show less

This isn't a company with a history to sell. It's a developer figuring things out in public — freelancing to pay the bills, and building this studio on the side as a place to learn, ship, and eventually turn into something bigger.

The name is a placeholder for where this is headed, not a claim about where it is now. Every page on this site gets updated as the skills, the projects, and the ambition grow — so what you're reading today is a snapshot, not a finished pitch.

Why solo

Why build this alone

Going solo wasn't the plan from day one — it's just where things naturally landed. Freelancing already meant working alone on client projects, so building a personal studio the same way felt like the honest extension of that. No co-founder to split decisions with, no investor to please — just direct control over what gets built and how.

The tradeoff is obvious: progress is slower than a full team, and some skills — art, sound, marketing — are still being picked up rather than outsourced. But everything that ships is fully understood end to end, and every mistake made along the way becomes a lesson that sticks instead of someone else's problem to fix.
