Why bother

Why write a devlog at all

It's easy to only show the finished, polished stuff. A devlog forces the opposite — writing down what's actually happening while it's happening, including the parts that don't work yet.

It's also a record for future reference: a way to look back later and see how a specific bug got fixed, why a decision was made, or how far things have actually come since the first messy prototype.
